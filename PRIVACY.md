# Privacy Policy — Docuready

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This plugin is a set of instructions (skills) that runs inside Claude. It has no servers of its own.

- **No data collection.** The plugin author does not receive, store or process your documents, reports or any other content.
- **What Claude reads.** Within your Claude session, Claude reads the files and drives you give it access to. Documents may contain personal or confidential data; the plugin instructs Claude to flag such files by name only and never to quote their content. Processing happens under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy and the terms of the storage service you connect.
- **No storage, no third parties.** Nothing is stored or sent anywhere by the plugin.
- **No tracking, cookies or analytics** are added by the plugin.
